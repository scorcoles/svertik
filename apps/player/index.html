svertik · player

The editor's engine with only what the scene needs: its effects load when the scene uses them (each one is a chunk of its own), and the fonts and the SVG importer only if they are needed.

How to embed it

<svg id="logo" data-svertik="scene.json"></svg>
<script type="module" src="svertik.js"></script>

Or from JavaScript, to control it:

import { play } from './svertik.js';

const player = await play(scene, '#logo');
player.stop();
